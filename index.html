About Us

Haux of Demvilux is a fashion brand and training academy located in Onitsha, Anambra State, Nigeria. We design elegant women's and children's clothing while training beginners and aspiring fashion designers in professional pattern drafting, cutting, and sewing.

Our Services

  • Women's Fashion
  • Children's Wear
  • Custom Sewing
  • Pattern Drafting
  • Fashion Training
  • Fashion Consultation

Our Vision

To become the biggest fashion brand and one of the most recognized fashion training academies in Africa.
